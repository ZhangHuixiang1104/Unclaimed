# 《不成双》美术资产清单与逐张制作提示词

## 统一制作纪律

画幅以 PC 检查台 1600×900 为基准。物件采用写实档案摄影而非绘本插画：旧黄麻、干草纤维、少量土色，不加鲜红绣球、姓名、地名、军徽、真实馆藏说明牌。所有鞋、绳为透明 PNG，正反面需为同一只鞋的连续形制；桌面、标尺、文字、放大圈和路线图用 CSS/SVG 完成。AI 不生成汉字，以免乱码和风格失控。

## 逐张生图提示词

**四只视图都在同一张中性摄影台的同一光照下生成，但每次只出一件透明物件。英文词可帮助模型稳定材质，不要出现文字。**

### 1. 较长鞋鞋面

> A single fictional 1930s-style Chinese handwoven straw sandal, larger adult size, viewed perfectly from above, toe pointing to the upper right at about 15 degrees. Coarse dry straw and yellow-brown hemp fibers, asymmetrical weaving, a restrained old repair on the outer heel made of a different dull fiber, frayed edges and natural wear, historically plausible handwork but not a replica of any museum artifact. Isolated complete object centered with generous clear margin, transparent alpha background, neutral soft museum conservation lighting, tactile high-detail photographic realism. No person, no feet, no red pompom, no labels, no written characters, no seal, no frame, no drop shadow baked into the alpha.

### 2. 较短鞋鞋面

> A single fictional 1930s-style Chinese handwoven straw sandal, visibly smaller and slightly narrower than a companion sandal, viewed perfectly from above, toe pointing to the upper left at about 12 degrees. Its weave is denser at the midfoot and a modest pale replacement braid repairs the toe, not the heel. Worn dry straw and brown hemp, hand-tied cord, realistic fiber irregularity. Isolated complete object centered with generous clear margin, transparent alpha background, same neutral conservation lighting and scale as a larger counterpart. No person, feet, red pompom, labels, written characters, museum tag, or baked shadow.

> The underside of the SAME larger fictional woven straw sandal: match elongated outline, outer-heel repair, toe shape, strap attachment and brown hemp texture of a companion top-view asset. Viewed perfectly from above as though carefully turned over; strongest abrasion at the outer back edge, a few compressed crossing fibers, no footprints or identifiable owner marks. Isolated complete object, transparent alpha, generous margin, neutral conservation photography, high detail. No text, no red decorations, no person, no card, no cast shadow baked into the image.

### 4. 较短鞋鞋底

> The underside of the SAME smaller fictional woven straw sandal: match narrow outline, pale toe replacement braid, toe shape and strap attachments of a companion top-view asset. Viewed perfectly from above after being turned over, with wear concentrated in the forefoot rather than the heel, visible compressed woven fibers. Isolated complete object, transparent alpha, generous margin, same scale and lighting as larger shoe. No text, no red decorations, no person, no card, no baked shadow.

### 5. 外绳

## 验收与替代方案

逐张检查：PNG 有真实透明 alpha，不是黑/白格背景；整鞋完整、没有多余鞋；四视图能被玩家认作两只同款式的正反面；较长/较短差异在不放大时可见；补编位置和鞋底磨损与剧情一致；无红绣球与 AI 汉字。如果鞋底和正面差异过大，前端不能直接叠为同一件，须重生该张或用实拍式原创 SVG/绘制转场避免假连续。
